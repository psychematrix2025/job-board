Job description

GovCIO, a software‑engineering services provider, is recruiting a full‑time, remote U.S. Software Engineer—AI Applications & ServiceNow. The position centers on crafting, building, deploying, operating, and maintaining secure, production‑grade applications for U.S. Government cloud environments that blend artificial intelligence, machine learning, and generative‑AI technologies. Candidates must be hands‑on developers capable of guiding AI‑enabled solutions from initial concept to production, collaborating with cross‑functional teams, adhering to strict security and compliance standards, and integrating with ServiceNow platforms as required. The engineer will also monitor application performance, troubleshoot incidents, and iterate on features using stakeholder input. Proficiency in DevOps practices such as CI/CD pipelines, containerization, and infrastructure‑as‑code, along with experience in Azure Government or AWS GovCloud, is essential, and strong communication, documentation, and teamwork across distributed environments are crucial.
