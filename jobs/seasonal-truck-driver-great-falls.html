Job description

CHS Inc., a prominent global agribusiness owned by U.S. farmers, ranchers, and cooperatives, delivers grain, food, and energy products to businesses and consumers worldwide. The company is headquartered across the United States and employs staff in 19 countries, focusing on creating connections that empower agriculture. Currently, CHS is recruiting a full‑time Seasonal Truck Driver to work out of Great Falls, Montana. The role involves transporting agricultural goods within the region, ensuring safe and timely deliveries, and maintaining accurate records of freight movements. Successful candidates will possess a valid commercial driver's license, a clean driving record, and experience handling commercial vehicles in a logistics environment. Flexibility, reliability, and a commitment to safety are essential for this position.
