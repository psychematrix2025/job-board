Job description

The Army Reserve Administrator position is a full‑time Military Technician role within the U.S. Army Reserve Command’s 807th Theater Medical Command, operating from the 983rd Medical Detachment of the 172nd Medical Battalion under the 330th Medical Brigade at Fort Snelling, Minnesota—though the arrangement is listed as remote. The posting specifies that candidates must hold an active Soldier membership in the Army Reserve prior to setting a start date. Interested applicants are directed to the Army Reserve’s recruitment portal at http://www.goarmy.com/reserve/jobs.html for minimum qualification details. The role is housed in the Fort Snelling area and requires traditional full‑time military technician duties consistent with the command’s medical support mission.
